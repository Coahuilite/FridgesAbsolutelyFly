[h1]These fridges are absolutely can FLY !![/h1]
[b]N.S.I.F Extend · F.A.F.[/b]

Following a review by the Nivarian engineering team, the floor is no longer classified as an essential refrigeration component.

Complete Project T.O.F.U, and fridges recognized by Nivarian Sleep In Fridges can hover and spin in place. As for the little dragon sleeping inside: asking the fridge to leave the floor is clearly more considerate than asking her to leave the fridge.

The T.O.F.U requisition asks only for ordinary steel and components. On the Nivarian household freezer, flotation also takes most of the shell's carrying weight out of the equation. The engineering team stresses that this concession does not extend to dinner, luggage or sleeping dragons.

[h2]The SPICE programme[/h2]
[b]Storage Preservation and Integrated Cooling Engineering[/b]

Through SPICE, the household freezer receives a series of staged improvements to preservation, continuity of power and ease of handling. Every project that sounds like a seasoning comes with a proper engineering report, real material requirements and a practical upgrade.

The acceptance criterion remains unchanged: wherever the equipment is placed, its contents should be kept in good condition.

[i]For RimWorld 1.6 / 1.7. Requires Nivarian Sleep In Fridges QWQ and its prerequisites. Odyssey is optional.[/i]

[h1]冰箱！当然！会飞！[/h1]
[b]涅瓦莲睡冰箱扩展 · F.A.F.[/b]

经涅瓦莲工程组复核，地板并不是制冷系统的必要零件。

完成 Project T.O.F.U 后，睡冰箱模组认可的冰箱便可原位悬浮，并按需旋转。至于睡在里面的小龙——让冰箱离开地板，显然比让她离开冰箱更尊重休息。

T.O.F.U 的物料申请仅列普通钢铁与零部件。涅瓦莲家用冷柜还能借此减轻大半柜壳的搬运负担。工程组特别说明：这项优待不包括晚饭、行李，或正在里面睡觉的小龙。

[h2]SPICE 计划[/h2]
[b]Storage Preservation and Integrated Cooling Engineering[/b]
储藏保鲜与一体化制冷工程。

通过 SPICE 计划，家用冷柜将接受一系列逐步展开的改良，进一步增强保鲜性能、供能连续性与搬运便利性。每个名字像调料的项目，都有正式的技术报告、实际材料需求，以及一项装得上去的改进。

验收标准保持不变：无论设备被放在哪里，里面的东西都应当保存妥当。

[i]适用于 RimWorld 1.6／1.7。需要 Nivarian Sleep In Fridges QWQ 及其前置；Odyssey 不是强制依赖。[/i]
